About Northbridge

Academic excellence with a global public purpose.

Northbridge International University is a community of scholars, creators, clinicians, engineers, and civic leaders dedicated to expanding knowledge and improving lives.

Mission

To educate principled leaders, advance discovery, and apply knowledge to the world’s most urgent challenges.

Vision

To stand among the world’s most respected universities through innovation, inclusion, and lasting impact.

History

Founded in 1898, Northbridge grew from a civic college into a global research university with campuses on four continents.
